Polycom VVX 300 Setup

Turning a Polycom VVX 300 into a shared desk phone that staff log in to with *50.

Access your phone's web interface by entering its IP address in a browser. You can find the IP on the phone screen: Settings > Status > Network > TCP/IP Parameters.

Before you start

Add the handset on the Desk phones page as a shared phone, named make + last 6 of its MAC (e.g. "Polycom 592E3A"). You'll need its login, password and screen page link from there. Wherever this guide says "phone login", use that login.

3 Show who's on the phone

The VVX 300 has no idle web page, so the screen saver does the job.

Click Save. The phone reboots once and registers with Twilio.

4 Check it works

After the phone reboots, check that it registered successfully:

  • On the phone screen: Settings > Status > Lines > Line 1 should show Registered
  • Dial *50: it should say nobody is logged in
  • Dial *50 + an extension to log someone in, then call that extension to check it rings
  • Set the phone's "Number when nobody's on it" on the Desk phones page

Troubleshooting

Line shows "Unregistered" Check the login and password match the Desk phones page. Verify Expires is set to 600 or higher.
Outbound works, inbound doesn't Check the Authentication Domain doesn't have a port suffix (e.g. :5060). This is the most common cause.
Audio cuts out or one-way audio Try disabling SIP ALG on your office router. Check that UDP ports 10000-20000 are not blocked.
423 "Interval too brief" Expires must be at least 600. Twilio rejects shorter registration intervals.
High latency / poor audio Ensure the Outbound Proxy and Line 1 Server use the Sydney edge (sip.sydney.twilio.com). Without it, traffic routes through the US.
