# Session Log — 2026-10-19

## Overview

Review of the first scheduled "Tina weekly check" email (week 2026-10-12 to 10-18), which raised one real problem:
8 inbound calls were left "ringing" in the call log. The cause was a sweep that only ran while someone had the phone
page open. Fixed in PR #35, merged and deployed the same morning (2026-10-19 03:57 UTC). The next weekly email is
the proof point.

## What shipped

- **PR #35 `sweep-stale-ringing`** (merge `ae1587d`, commit `5918143`) — the stale-ringing sweep now runs from the
  5-minute `/api/queue/cleanup` cron.
  - New `CallLogMixin.sweep_stale_ringing()`. It runs before `cleanup_old_queued_calls`, because the sweep skips
    callers still waiting in `queued_calls`.
  - The cron's activity log now says "Closed N stale ringing calls."
  - Verified live: the 04:00 UTC cron run logged "Closed 0 stale ringing calls", which is the expected result in
    business hours.
  - Tested against an in-memory table: an old inbound ringing call becomes `ended_early`, an outbound one becomes
    `missed`, and a recent call or a queued-waiting caller is left alone.

## What the weekly check found

- Incoming: 795 of 795 OK.
- Outgoing: 11 of 30 unanswered calls were recorded as answered (1.5%, inside the 2% tolerance). The five listed
  examples were all from 12–13 Oct, before PR #31's fix.
- **Problem: 8 unfinished incoming calls.** All 8 were after-hours or weekend inbound calls (mostly Canberra The Hub,
  one Batemans Bay, one Wagga) where the caller hung up during the welcome or menu. They stayed `ringing` with no
  `ended_at` until 2026-10-18 20:07:26 UTC (Mon 7:07am Sydney). That is 7 minutes after the 20:00 UTC cross-check,
  when the first page load ran the sweep and stamped them `ended_early`. So the report was right to flag them and
  the cause was the sweep's trigger, not the calls.

## Decisions

- Scope kept to the ringing sweep only.
- The crude "answered for more than 2h → completed" sweep was NOT moved into the cron. It sets `ended_at = now`,
  so it needs more thought before it runs unattended.

## Issues

- Root cause: `STALE_RINGING_SWEEP_SQL` ran only from `CallLogMixin.get_active_calls` and `close_stale_calls`, i.e.
  only while a phone page was open. Off-hours nobody opens it, so nothing swept.
- Six of the eleven outbound mismatches were not listed in the email, so they were not checked individually.
- The local rinq clone's `main` was stale (at `2e67834`) against origin. This session read `origin/main`
  (`ae1587d`) for the worktree.

## Discoveries

- When a cross-check flag looks fine on inspection, filter `call_log.updated_at` to after the check's run time. That
  finds what changed after the check ran (here, the 20:07 UTC sweep).
