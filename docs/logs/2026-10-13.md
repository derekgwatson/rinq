## What shipped

- **PR #22 `handoff-button-reset`** (phone.html only, +16 lines, commit 85c5d2e):
  - A warm transfer to a colleague is completed with the main-panel **Hand off** button (`#btnHandoff`).
    `completeTransfer()` disables it and labels it "Completing...". The success path never put it back.
    a successful hand-off ends the call and the page was expected to be done with it.
  - The next warm transfer on the same page load therefore showed a dead, unpressable "Completing..." button.
    Agents had to refresh the page to get a working one.
  - Fix: new `resetHandoffButton()`, called from `resetCallUI()` and when a warm consult starts.
